# MDEditor sample

Move the cursor between lines: the line you are editing shows raw Markdown,
every other line is rendered.

## Inline formatting

Text with **bold**, *italic*, ***both***, ~~strikethrough~~, ==highlight== and `inline code`.
Escaped \*asterisks\* stay literal, and so does snake_case_name.

Links: [Qt](https://www.qt.io), https://github.com and a [[wiki link]] (Ctrl+click to open).
